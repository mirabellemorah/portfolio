Who am I?

Life can be quite interesting and I believe in curiosity, playfulness, experimentation and innovation.

I think of my work as results and answers to business problems. Typically I work closely with marketing, growth and design teams. I research into the problems or jobs a user needs accomplished. And depending on my tasks, I develop data and user-centered communications and marketing strategies; develop front-end codes or create motion designs. As a full-stack marketer and creative developer I help startups, social enterprises and impact-led changemakers solve comms, design and business challenges. Presently, the value and reach of my work cuts across across Africa, Europe and North America.

To get a clearer sense of how I'll collaborate with you and support your work, feel free to explore my past client projects and if you'd like to be part of that story, I'd love to hear from you! 👋🏽😉

On the side of impact, I've always been fascinated by the power of storytelling and digital media. I founded the award-winning BlankPaperz Media at 17, growing it from 0-100k web users and 200 contributing writers. I've trained 100s of young people on personal leadership, communications and creative entrepreneurship whilst supporting education via GC Funds. Some startups and social enterprises I've worked with include Rabatta and the Social Enterprise World Forum. Organisations I've been affiliated with include the Mastercard Foundation, Chatham House (The Royal Institute for International Affairs), Stand.Earth, SAP and National Geographic Society. Currently, I'm building Grohwie Resource Library which houses 400+ marketing, communications and design resources. Plus building the Design Fellowship (over 165+ applicants per cohort) to support junior designers with the soft and business skills they need to thrive, all tailored to their needs.
