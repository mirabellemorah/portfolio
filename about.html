About

I believe in curiosity, playfulness, experimentation and innovation.

I make motion designs, comms strategy and write lines of code. I’m in the business of moving mindsets, helping people understand and trust ideas or products that should matter. I study my audience, develop communications strategies and motion designs based on their behavior. Call me a creative technologist.

I work closely with marcomms, design and growth teams because I focus on growing communities, revenue, and a finer world. My true north has always been to amplify impactful stories, ideas and grow them to reach millions. I see my work and results as answers to social and business problems. Presently, the value and reach of my work cut across across Africa, Europe and North America.

To get a clearer sense of how I'll collaborate with you and support your work, explore my past client projects, my experiments and send me an email! 👋🏽

On the side of social impact, I founded the award-winning BlankPaperz Media at 17, growing it from 0-100k web users and 200 contributing writers. I've trained hundreds of young people on leadership, communications and creative entrepreneurship, given N1.7m supporting education and spoken to thousands of people. Amongst numerous awards, I'm a Skoll Fellow and Mastercard Foundation Scholar. I have an MSc in Entrepreneurship and Innovation from the University of Edinburgh.

Some startups and social enterprises I've worked with include Rabatta and the Social Enterprise World Forum; Chatham House (The Royal Institute for International Affairs), Stand.Earth, SAP and National Geographic Society. Currently, I'm building Grohwie Resource Library which is a live database of design and product tools. I also lead the Grohwie Design Fellowship to mentor the next generation of tech design leaders in business and soft skills.
