These below, are a collection of quotes I read and heard. They resonated with me and I share them with you.

- “Great value precedes great wealth. If you want to make millions, impact millions. Be a fiduciary to millions and you won’t be useless.” — [UNSCRIPTED: Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Entrepreneurship (MJ DeMarco)](https://amzn.eu/d/1QP1n7A)
- Money’s hot pursuit actually repels money. However, flip the magnet and forget about money and focus on value. And the magnets attract. So how can you polarise the money scam and attract value vouchers? First, understand how value vouchers move. To get to the money vouchers: Offer Value (product/service creation £). Let the product or service have its perceived value communicated to another party (marketing and messaging). Thirdly, have a mutual agreement and an equilibrium with that party (closing). Then have actual value honourably delivered (execution). — [UNSCRIPTED: Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Entrepreneurship (MJ DeMarco)](https://amzn.eu/d/1QP1n7A)
- “The value of a man should be seen in what he can give and not what he receives” — Albert Einstein
- “Not in rushing and striving in the material plane, but on the battlefield of the soul are these things won.” —[ God calling devotional, Jan 30](https://www.twolisteners.org/?p=7229).
- “A star shines the brightest at its last moments” — Someone. One of its meanings denotes to a saying that often in the last moments of a person’s life, they may seem the happiest, before taking their lives (such as a person suffering from depression, etc.).
- “Fortune favours the prepared”. — Vandal Savage, Young Justice
- “See everything as a gift from God and in the good and the bad praise God. Every day is an opportunity. Learn the lesson, and be thankful.” — Mirabelle
- “For it is not in success where true character is revealed but in failure.” — Black Manta, Young Justice
- “Smart woman. Don’t ever be too polite and too helpful. Always follow your instinct.” — Someone’s comment on Instagram
- “High-quality work = time spent x quality of focus” —   
  James Scholz ([How I studied for 12 hours a day for over a year](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kICh_d6tHQk))
- “The world is full of magical things patiently waiting for our senses to grow sharper.” — W.B. Yeats
- “Respect your own process. What works for others might not work for you. God is a personal God. He works in each of us uniquely. So learn to focus on your own path as God is also doing mighty things in your story. Avoid comparing. Stop putting blame on others. Start planting seeds of genuine kindness instead of hidden hatred. Other people’s lives might look great, but yours is just getting better and better. God is moving. Trust Him.” — [Jonalyn](https://www.instagram.com/joenasandiego/?hl=en)
- “A human being is a part of the whole called by us universe, a part limited in time and space. He experiences himself, his thoughts and feeling as something separated from the rest, a kind of optical delusion of his consciousness. This delusion is a kind of prison for us, restricting us to our personal desires and to affection for a few persons nearest to us. Our task must be to free ourselves from this prison by widening our circle of compassion to embrace all living creatures and the whole of nature in its beauty.” — Albert Einstein
