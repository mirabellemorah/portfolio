I just wrote about my [creative coding workshop](https://medium.com/@mirabellemorah/my-experience-at-coding-systems-creative-coding-workshop-and-intl-12e5da65d10c) experience with creative systems. As the year progresses, I’ve been playing and experimenting with how things appear and how we can turn a simple loaf of bread into a full bread salad. In other words, I’m exploring how to combine different elements in new ways.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1440/1*s6gjbvhKnuL2gKHV3wU5wA.png)

I picked up block shapes and pixel shapes for inspiration during the workshop and a bit before it. I was exploring how halftones, pixel shapes, and turbulent displace and play together. I was also exploring how you can take one piece of design or inspiration and turn it into something with multiple meanings. While I haven’t put this into mockups yet, I can only imagine what they would look like in a mockup setting, which I think I would love to explore. Actually, let’s do that. Let me show you what it would look like in a random unedited mockup.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/2160/1*KelbKwkb_Kf82Yz2ud_ZAg.jpeg)

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1080/1*5abTi-UIn9sF-qw5nGD5gg.gif)

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1080/1*2wsMqzVruvgXBSAM_35LUw.gif)

That being said, this was my first inspiration. I picked this up from Pinterest, and it reminded me of Avatar, the Fire Nation. 

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1440/1*2DPgrxfoRUdf-U_Z8lwdhg.jpeg)

Then I used an iPad animation software, created my version.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1440/1*3pUnsKczK0oJRPpS95SYFQ.gif)

Then I animated it frame by frame, and used turbulence, halftones, and pixels to come up with different designs. 

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1080/1*NbaFlM8NqhdM_y5A1UnYQQ.gif)

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1080/1*WmRU9diZMO9ld2XZ0XuDag.gif)

I also tried to explore timing: I spaced them frame by frame, meeting on twos and some on threes. Enjoy.
