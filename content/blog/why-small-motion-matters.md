Think of a ball dropping on the floor. It does not just stop. It squashes, bounces and settles.

Good interface motion works the same way. When a button gives a tiny press and a soft return, you know it heard you.

**Try this today:** pick one button on your site and give it a 200 millisecond ease. Then ask someone if it feels different. They usually say yes, even if they cannot say why.
