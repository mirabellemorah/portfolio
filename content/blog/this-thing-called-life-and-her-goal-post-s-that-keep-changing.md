For me, I strongly believe that the goalpost keeps changing. I’ve recently asked myself what it is that I really wanted out of life (I mean I am constantly asking this). And none of the things that really came to my soul were about money. Again, I started to ask myself,

> “Are you sure?”,

> “But why am I asking myself? Am I sure?”

I think that we’re in a very hyper money-focused world, and let’s not be delusional. I have a business degree and I run a design business myself, so if there’s anyone to tell me that money is important, it’s me. Cash flow is extremely important, but everything else I’ve wanted from the deepest part of my soul just had **nothing** to do with money (because it’s in abundance and a byproduct of value).

### Talentedness

On the topic of talents, I’m a multi-talented person and I don’t think that my skills are spread wide. If you ask me what it is that I can do at my true core, it’s this: ***that in everything I do, I just want to give people access to new opportunities, to help them get into rooms and access things that they either desire to have or deserve to be in. That’s me.***

Even when I was running BlankPaperz, I was amplifying the stories of people because I wanted them to get access to readers who could read about them and also contact them. I’m running Grohwie, and I am also doing the same thing right now with founders. I’m making motion demo videos so that new people can get to learn about them through quick videos, especially in a world full of noise. Maybe my videos also add to that noise, but maybe this noise will catch your attention.

I am also helping emerging designers find business opportunities for themselves, and that’s my perspective on lots of things.

But againsomewhere out there in the world is someone who is just mono-talented, telling us that it’s bad to have different talents or to know how to do different things, that we should focus on just one thing because that one thing will carry us far. I don’t really want my life to work like that, based on someone else’s imagination of how the world should be. I think it should be different. And I want my life to be different. And it is different.

So yeah, I think the goalpost keeps changing for many things. Today they say, “***Become a digital marketer, you have the job of the future.***” Now it’s ***“learn software.”*** Tomorrow it’s ***“AI.”***

Everything keeps changing, but you have to hold yourself accountable, hold yourself steady, and remind yourself of what you truly want, daily. No matter what goalpost keeps changing, no matter what new metrics come out, you’re solid within yourself.

Also, learn to make money. Trust me, or learn to multiply money and invest it.
