I just really want to stop being so performative. God, please, *abeg*, me I’m so exhausted and tired. Like, so tired. Of the chasing, of the tinkering with my creative outputs based on what I think people would think. I used to quote this thing as a teenager, that “*an artist does not tailor his art according to those around him but he pours it out just as it is within him.*” Someone said it, I don’t 100% remember who and no, I’m not going to google this.

So what happened Mira? What happened to you and why did you start tailoring everything, outside of work, to people?

Mhmm *nwanne’m* if you ask me, *na how me I wan take answer you?* I used to be such a solid writer, I wrote with such flair and in beautiful figures of speech. Now the only things I write are:

*“How you can be productive in 4 million ways”*

*“10 tips for crushing your dreams”*

And all those other funky things. I’m tired of the marketing writing. I just want to write for myself. But I’m also conscious when I write for myself, and yet want to share it online. I don’t want to share everything online. So I still hold myself back. And I don’t even want to do that.

That’s just a metaphor for the rest of my life. Many days honestly, I’m just genuinely tired, I’m exhausted, tired of chasing. I am grateful, I feel content, I want to do the many things in my head, I welcome peace, I dine with good success and favour from above. Some days, I’m just humanly tired and overstimulated and wondering why?

I just really want to repent. From all this performance. I’ll write the way my 17 year old heart once wrote, freely and silly and imaginative. And I’ll create just like the designer in me enjoys to recreate from nature.

\#sigh… I’m a business student, I own a business. I love art. These two sides, sometimes collide.
