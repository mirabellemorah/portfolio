## #1: learning to animate — in 5 days

I’m always in total awe of people who make frame by frame animations and *oof!* They feel magical to me. Kinda always felt like traditional animation was a bit intimidating (much less than motion design) and no matter what course or video I watched, it always seemed like it wasn’t enough or the direction wasn’t clear.

I mean when it comes to animation/motion design I’m a [keyframe](http://www.grohwie.com) kinda girl! But frame by frame really has caught my heart and after years of being a motion designer and animating in after effects, I’m expanding my queendom into this.

### Finding the perfect animation course

I had a few courses in mind but I ended up going for [The Complete Introduction to 2D Animation – Full Package](https://stringbing.gumroad.com/l/Introto2DComplete?layout=profile) by Toniko Pantoja. I got it 9 days ago but I’ve quite only been practicing traditional animation with Toniko’s course for perhaps 5–6 days in total so far. I’m pretty much still in the chapter 2, exploring principles of animation (once again) and below are my notes and thoughts.

#### **Day 1**

I was mostly getting a grasp of lots of stuff, learning terminologies and practicing timing and binge watching lots of indie animations.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1440/1*5067_vICV6RdBng3xIGW4g.gif)

Timing

#### Day 2

Started properly on looking squishy objects and squashing them. This is stuff I do with the value graph in after effects for motion design but just doing this traditionally kinda extended more of that knowledge.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1440/1*L7nyMuthi8Dklm2SS33j1Q.gif)

Squish and squash

#### Day 3

I got ambitious, and I went on to draw a frame by frame pencil animation of a scene from Warner Bros Animation’s Thundercats Roar — animation by Studio Yotta ([which I found onf FramexFrame](https://framexframe.tumblr.com/)).

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1440/1*dhiiBNw83em8JzD-366gpQ.gif)

My pencil entry for a reanimation of Thundercats

I had to also learn S anc C curves, learn arches, waves, secondary action and make flags on Day 3. This was by far one very challenging exercise I had to do. Wow, I did this for 2 days, drawing and retrying and cleaning. But that’s what I’ve learned about learning, you just gotta keep trying different things until something works. You can clearly see the progression I had.

By this time, I moved unto bouncing balls and moving them into an arch, utilising squish and squash and secondary action. It was a pretty simple exercise, so I turned a bit cinematic with it. All these are still part of practicing the principles of animations.

#### Day 5

I make a lot of brand designs so I looked into making mixed media style for a fictional hiking and nature based company. Then I also tried to draw characters and animate them frame by frame too, it’s still the 5th day, an ambitious move, different face perspective/positions and hand still needed some work and I’ll improve on my drawing of those. Hands are sooo tricky yeah?! But it was at this point that I realised that there are different kinds of brushes, I was having difficulties using the paintbucket on some of my shapes no matter how much I closed the shapes, until I switched to using a vector brush.

Speed drawing time! I tried to set a 10 mins timer to sketch and draw this but let’s just say 10+9 = 19. I spent 19 minutes getting proportions and trying to draw with 0 onion skins.
