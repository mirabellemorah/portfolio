## Problem

There wasn’t really a problem, more of one of those sad life events that just inspired me. The origin story of this video is that someone whom I hadn’t spoken to in so many years reached out, and told me many fake stories to get money from me. Then once I sent him some cash to supposedly help, he stopped calling me, didn’t say he’d received it, went silent and I never heard from him again.
