When you keep going you’ll find what you’ve been searching for… *If you keep going, it will find you.*

When I first opened AfterEffects in 2021 or 2022 to [learn motion design](https://www.instagram.com/miras.view?igsh=ZXF5cGVpNnVhZmpk), the whole interface was scary. But in my spare times I have kept learning, replicating different illustration styles, trying to mimic them and add my own flair.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*5J25Y8V_Iv9S1t6UPTOSSw.gif)

When I work I want to play, to be patient with my progress. It’s really frustrating when the codes I write to develop websites or the drag and drops I am trying to work on, on WebFlow, flop. Special thanks to those who make a million YouTube videos and answer questions on stack overflow and other websites.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*ld5NvZ_SgclT12FTT7eq0w@2x.jpeg)  
*My leaderboard score on Mimo*

I can now see the rewards of my months of finding time to make motion designs and code. I picked up [coding in the summer of 2024](https://x.com/MirabelleMorah/status/1807434551418822908) (uh… 2–3 months ago) when I went back to Nigeria for [research](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C9fgezDgPKx/?igsh=NHk5ZW51ZGtxcmZi) on my masters dissertation, to build a product and business idea, [Grohwie](https://www.grohwie.com). I found coding quite interesting and challenging to my brain and I tell you, it seems like learning css, html and javascript is one of the most useful skills I have picked up of late because a lot of other things have begun to light up and make more sense.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*gRftxH7PPFwEHMwMSAUejw.jpeg)  
*This is the first site I developed and a link to it on GitHub .*

I now understand how to make [expressions better for animating in After Effects](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/cool-stuff-i-took-steps-created-motion-design-chatgpt-mirabelle-morah-tmbse?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios&utm_campaign=share_via). I understand more about websites and frontend designs. I am not fully clear on backends yet but I am getting there.

In march 2024 I was terribly confused about what building digital platforms took — I don’t mean building on Wordpress or Shopify. I mean building digital products that aren’t exactly locked in to a vendor. Do I use no-code tools? Do I code and develop it myself? Do I pay a professional? How difficult is it to learn the basics that I need? I am blessed to have good friendships with very resourceful people that I could ask questions of. And I will keep bringing people who are better than me on board my projects and goals.

Around August — September 2024 I tried to set up a webflow site for [Grohwie.com](https://www.grohwie.com). I tell you you I have been trying to get a hang of Webflow for the last couple of years. They say it’s no code but Webflow has been proving tough for me to hack (not literally). The more I learn about css functionalities the better it gets for me on Webflow. But ooof… 😅 Webflow though.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*NiSjKNrIhH0XG-WefrPWZQ.gif)  
*Grohwie’s first “coming soon” website*

I struggled a bit to design something nice, an alright “coming soon” landing page for Grohwie on Webflow but after finishing that, it was looking out of place to me 🥲.

It’s October now, and last night I remembered that I have actually developed 2 websites in the last few months as part of Code First Girls kickstarter course. [One website was for fun](https://miramorah.github.io/girlcode/) and the other [was a group project](https://github.com/Miramorah/beyonce-CFG). I whipped back up my first fully developed website and made something off of it.

I already have the brand design for Grohwie, so I further animated it on After Effects.

Went into VSCode and modified pieces of it and used claude.ai to answer some of my coding questions.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*JlZCUhzndT6dU1sAHgHWbw@2x.jpeg)  
*After Effects + VS Code + Github*

Last night I found it difficult to push from VS Code to Github — stage, commit, add remote, push and all. I still don’t know what a remote is but after a while I finally did it!

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*hnkphg1E2eLyXdEwjXK6UA@2x.jpeg)

Then I connected Grohwie’s domain name to Github after successfully pushing my edits from VSCode to Git. This is what my landing page to tell you that Grohwie is coming soon, looks like. Cool huh?

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*JLW11YaK8834osLwIlZ2KA@2x.jpeg)

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*bBHzp8MX9bLIrPtroAJ9yw.gif)  
*Updated website for Grohwie*

With coding, learning, growth and life in general. You’ve just got to keep moving. In between all this coding and motion design, I’ve been a full time msc entrepreneurship and innovation student in a global top 30 university. I’ve volunteered for friends, I have been researching about why global aid doesn’t stop poverty, how organizations can improve their design and comms output, how innovation can be managed in context, etc. I wasn’t always editing code and animations, there has been life in between for me to do too, like seeing how I can add value for under-served populations in Nigeria.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*qlDDgmPptg1ZprYoxaviRw@2x.jpeg)  
*The night I felt like a genius! Writing code, designing motion graphics, publishing Grohwie’s “coming soon” page on Github after a good many bugs :)*

If you keep going you will find what you’ve been searching for. And if you keep going, what you’re searching for will also find you.

*P.s Did you know I witnessed an aurora for the first time in my life, from my bedroom a few nights ago! It was so cool!! And now that has been ticked off my bucket list. No need to travel to Norway or Iceland to see it again 😉. Thank you God and lady universe.*
