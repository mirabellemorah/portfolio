Sometimes, the most effective email marketing strategies are the simplest ones. Just community care and clarity.

I have an email marketing strategy to get participants to register for any of your events. It works on me too.

## This is it: don’t just sell events in emails. Sell help and care.

- send a super short email
- 1–2 short paragraphs max
- use as much plain text as possible
- little graphic design
- personalized to the sender
- JUST one CTA
- don’t advertise the event
- rather ask how you can help
- and sign off with your name.

## **Example**:

*From: Mira from Grohwie*

*Subject: How can I support you? //Or // Need some support Linda?*

*Body:*

*Hi Linda \[participant’s name\] 👋*

*How have you been? I’m Mira and I saw you attended* [Grohwie](https://www.linkedin.com/article/edit/7336138731134889984/#) *Summit last year. I was wondering, is there any way I can support you this year if you’d like to be back, to meet new people and attend this year’s summit again? Why don’t you let me know. I’ll love to hear from you about this (just send me an email!) and I’ll find ways to support you or answer any questions you have.*

*Cheers!*

*Mira, Lead,* [Grohwie](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grohwie/)

Once you do this, be ready with a discount code. Be ready to respond to a lot of emails! Be ready to show participants you care and are ready to build a community with them.

In the last 4 years I have experimented with designing and sending hundreds of marketing emails to keep people informed, or get thousands of people to register for an annual international event that changes country location every year. I’ve also achieved as much as 74% open rate compared to 45% for the industry standard using this method.
