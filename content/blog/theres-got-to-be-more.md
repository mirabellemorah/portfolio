We are tired of the status quo  
There has got to be more to life than  
Us Us Us  
School School School  
Work Work Work  
There has got to be more to life than our eyes can meet

We are tired of contemplating what  
People would  
Say Say Say  
We would rather think of what  
God God God  
Has to say  
And do what's  
Right Right Right

Life is too big to be all and only about our selfish interests.
