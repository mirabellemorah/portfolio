I was scared before doing this. Then I did it any way.

> *“This whole program for me \[has given me\] a whole new perspective and approach to my career.” –* [*Grohwie Design Fellow*](https://www.grohwie.com/fellowship)

## Goal

For the longest time, I’ve wanted to host a residency program, like something in-person.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*hlg6Lfc-tm9g2U7hkaA2Cw@2x.jpeg)

I admire The [Kuta Artist-in-Residence](https://www.instagram.com/kutanigeria/?hl=en) fellowship, and I hope someday I can do something similar in the digital space for young creative tech humans in not-too-famous cities across Nigeria, the UK, and other African countries.

The journey of a thousand miles begins with a step and a spark. And my first step was organising the [Grohwie](https://www.grohwie.com/) Design Fellowship, a 1 month international Design Fellowship for emerging designers (branding, web, digital). The initial cohort was virtual, subsequent cohorts will be in-person.

The goal of the fellowship to support emerging designers who have 1–2 years of professional experience, with soft and business skills to be better.

## How it started

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*o38bJpLa0ZEJZ4NE9pLsAA@2x.jpeg)

I bootstrapped the majority of the fellowship’s cost; a little spilled over grant cash from Mastercard Foundation/Edinburgh Innovations in the past year, and support from a friend helped me onboard an 11th design fellow (my initial plan was for 10).

I opened applications first in Nigeria. 165 applications came in. Then [11](https://www.instagram.com/p/DITSDuSMy68/) Grohwie Design Fellows were selected.

## Next steps

I created a 4-week curriculum tailored to the challenges these incredible humans said they faced in their applications. Then I invited 5 top, expert facilitators (2 international creative agency [founders](https://www.instagram.com/p/DIlN3xssUGd/) and [directors](https://www.instagram.com/p/DIlKMVPsnUe/), 1 [senior](https://www.instagram.com/p/DIwc5tsMkXr/) product designer at Intercom, 1 [multidisciplinary](https://www.instagram.com/p/DILUOM_Mucf/) design freelancer, and [me](https://www.instagram.com/p/DIQs2pssAJX/), a motion designer and creative developer) to speak to them.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*5WswEcnsScofhQx2zoRsPA@2x.jpeg)

## Challenges and stuff I’ll do different next time

*What are my “I’ll never do this again” moments?*

### 1. Plan differently

I spent a lot of time over-planning.

Setting up a roadmap and strategy are very important, but some things cannot be anticipated by planning alone. I needed to take action so I could see the next step.

### 2. Friends vs professionals

I wanted to work quickly and with people I already knew. So I searched my contact list and reached out to individuals I felt could do the work I needed done.

This isn’t something I would 100% rely on in the future again.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*utQh6AAl5Awf_CFGvkVXIA@2x.jpeg)

I could have done a bit more vetting by putting out an application and then inviting people to apply for internal team member roles.

With my initial strategy of reaching out directly to a contact and sending them a proposal (without thorough vetting), I had to change a team member a few days into the fellowship, then ran a few interviews and found someone else who did an excellent job for the next 3–4 weeks.

### 3. Where’s plan C and D?

My last facilitator never responded – actually she responded 3 months later. Though I was softly disappointed and worried, I thank God she didn’t respond on time.

I guess next time, I could have more backup facilitators and send facilitator invites no less than a month ahead.

You see, when she didn’t respond, I was forced to put out a call for facilitator recommendations on my Instagram stories and [ÌníOlúwa Abíódún](https://www.linkedin.com/in/inioluwa-abiodun/), a senior product designer in Intercom, London, volunteered to share her story and come teach the fellows a thing or two. And I strongly believe it was divinely orchestrated.

![Grohwie Design Fellows cohort 1](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*VTr7taE3dFJJEFlnljc3xg@2x.jpeg)  
*Grohwie Design Fellows (cohort 1) with ìníolúwa abíódún*

If I am being honest, I always felt Ini was way busy and far beyond my reach, so I was super excited she offered to be a speaker. And as a matter of fact, her session was rated the best by all fellows.

> “The final session of the design fellowship was the most impactful for me. Inioluwa was an inspiring speaker who went beyond surface-level topics and shared deep, practical insights on growth as a designer.

> One phrase that really stood out was: ‘Being a creative is more than just creativity.’ It sparked a mindset shift for me – reminding me that design isn’t just about visuals, but about how we think, lead, and solve real problems. Her session gave me a renewed sense of purpose in how I approach my work as a designer.” – Fellow

## Successes

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*VAyzZURdEKckpi8_3YldDw@2x.jpeg)

> “This was like a perspective shift for me, I was shown a whole other new world of possibilities. Hearing the stories of these important people made me realize that I can do all that I set my mind to. This whole program for me, \[has given me\] a whole new perspective and approach to my career.” — Fellow

That was the feedback a fellow gave.

Would I run this fellowship again? **Absolutely yes and many times over!** As God’s seed, time and harvest remain, I will always be a blessing and bring solutions to people’s needs.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*V8K9qaHM0oj8PAIiQ5yApg@2x.jpeg)

For people who decide to apply to fellowships and programs: one success advice I would give to future applicants would be to make sure their portfolio links are working.
