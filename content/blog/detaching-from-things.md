As of the moment I am writing this, barely 32 hours ago I worked on my second Social Enterprise World Forum (SEWF) Policy Forum and Kasia, who’s been the communications manager after me complimented the engaging way I responded to social media comments during the event. For context, I used to be comms manager for SEWF before handing over to Kasia, now I manage comms in a different portfolio still under SEWF.

*As a fun fact, the Policy Forum is run online with team members from different parts of the world.* [*Take a look*](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sewfcic_sewf-policy-forum-2024-behindthescreens-activity-7254778600439123969-Xq93?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios) *behind the scenes at some of the workspaces in Spain, UK, Portugal, The Netherlands and South Africa.*

When Kasia gave the compliment, I thought about it for a second and I knew why I made a comment that sounded engaging and not robotic. It was because I was detached. Not in an aloof way, it’s just a better kind of detaching.

With life these days, I find myself detaching from a high, false sense of wanting things to be perfect. I am detaching every day from overthinking. And as I do this I give myself permission to be quicker, to be creative, to be myself and enjoy my moments.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*l8WLz43NlnrespDgXTqY1A@2x.jpeg)

I am always excellent in my delivery and the works of my hands are excellent. But I don’t want to overthink things and become paralyzed in my over analyses. If a piece of work will not be accepted by a group of people then it will just not be accepted. My detachment allows me to do the best and highest work that I can, to accept that my desire to have it accepted is valid but it also may not be. It allows me not take outcomes personally but yet to be optimistic that I have grown in my capacity and that there are millions of others who might accept my output. Or better still that I could receive feedback to improve.

In a nutshell I believe the concept that best describes my feelings, is the concept of high intentions and low attachments. It isn’t a lackadaisical approach to life. It is one that I like to assume says “Seeth thou a man diligent in his ways. He will stand before kings and not mere men. + lay your burdens down at the feet of the Lord.”

I do my best both in the good and the bad times. And I give praise in both seasons, never taking things personally, never overthinking.
