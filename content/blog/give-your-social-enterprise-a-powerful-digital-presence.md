**Imagine this:** I am a potential donor who is moved by your cause. I’m shaking with excitement to support your mission because someone randomly talked about it. You see, I am ready to contribute — but I can’t find your website. Or worse, what I’m looking at is an outdated page that doesn’t capture the transformational work you’re doing. Shame. Now you’ve left me in a limbo of uncertainty.

In today’s digital world, a social enterprise without a good online presence is like trying to change the world with one hand tied behind your back — except your work doesn’t need much online presence and is heavy on-ground, that’s alright too if that’s where your target audience is at.

## Why it matters

Think of your website as your organization’s digital headquarters — a space where your mission, impact, and story come alive 24/7. It’s not just about having a website, you’re using this digital platform to amplify your social impact and engage your community.

Going national or global? Here’s what will make your social enterprise’s website truly impactful:

## Your impact stories

- Showcase real stories of the people you’ve been assisting or systems you’ve been influencing
- Show this impact with downloadable reports and even visually with motion design

## Add links to social media and include a newsletter subscription button

- So you can build an online community around your cause and keep them updated
- Supporters can comment and engage with your work
- Regular updates via email and social media will open opportunities for collaboration and partnership

## Include a “how to get involved” page

- So people can connect or donate
- And you can facilitate volunteer sign-ups and involvement

### Dedicate a page or two to the incredible humans working those engines

- Go beyond the usual *“who’s on the team”* tactic and include videos or short blurbs about the daily activities of some staff working hard to accomplish tasks
- It humanises your efforts

## The bottom line is that …

In the world of social enterprise, your website is more than a digital presence — it’s a platform for change. It’s where passion meets purpose, where stories inspire action, and where supporters become partners in your mission.
