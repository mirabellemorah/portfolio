*You said you would miss her  
She said she knew you would  
You said you were horny  
And she asked if that was all you thought about her  
It pains me so much to write that  
YOU HURT HER*

Your planet revolved around her  
She was the axial force that kept you in place  
You treasured her but still  
YOU HURT HER

She asked if she were your sex object or symbol  
She wanted to mean so much more in your eyes  
She wanted you to see her as a gazelle by the spring side  
Graceful loving and breathtaking  
She wanted you to treat her like a woman  
With respect and dignity  
But yesterday  
YOU HURT HER

Mirabelle Morah
