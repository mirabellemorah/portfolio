I used to be so scared of reaching out to people. Cold messaging and emailing people.

Then when I got desperate and broke, I don’t know how but I started to LOVE reaching out to people to create awareness of what I was building.

Necessity like they say is the mother of invention and in my case, motivation.

I didn’t care anymore who ignored me or tried to low ball me etc. I didn’t just care like that anymore.

I used to see roles like business development associate and partnerships as big scary things.

To be honest, in my daily life I was already doing those job descriptions in ways I didn’t know.

I am very good at nurturing relationships. I’ve made friends with investors whom I didn’t know were investors (as at the time of my conversation with them and that they were) investing big cash (I just wasn’t seeking money). While talking to them, suddenly they would be pointing me in directions of how they started their investment journeys.

I like to imagine that including God’s favor, I was also interested in just understanding them as normal humans and what made them tick. Not for what they had. Baby! People smell this thing from afar!

I once told someone from a VC firm who was only investing from \$500k upwards that I would love to connect them with a friend, and they gave me alternatives instead because my friend was raising less than what they gave out, 50k.

## **Books that helped**

Also, very randomly I had been reading books like:

1.  “Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion” by Robert B Cialdini
2.  “Never Split the Difference: Negotiating as If Your Life Depended on It” by Chris Voss
3.  And Bob Burg’s 3 book Set (Go-Givers Sell More, The Go-Giver Leader, The Go-Giver) — I only had 2 of these.

In addition, I already worked in digital marketing and communication strategy.

So when necessity made me start a lot of cold emailing (I was still shaky about cold calling), everything began to blossom like a beautiful hibiscus bud.

I would never forget what [Kenneth Igbomor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kenneth-igbomor-b4915417/) said to me years ago when we both won the [Ooni of Ife’s Royal African Young Leadership Award](https://www.instagram.com/p/CPgXEE3Alos/?img_index=1):

> “Make friends before you need them.”

I guess in the same fashion, learn as much as you can before you need them.

This life is tasty and strange and exciting.
