> Lessons on how to show your work

1.  Document every day as much as possible.
2.  Unashamedly share the work that you do with others.
3.  In sharing your work, look at it more from a process perspective than a product perspective. What is your process like? What are some of your behind-the-scenes frustrations? Who is inspiring your work?
4.  Put out more of your work online.
5.  Having your own website and collecting emails will serve you on the long run, but remember to ask for permission before adding people to your mailing list.
6.  Always credit people and put a link back to their work if they inspired your work! It’s the least we can do.
7.  Talk about the stuff you do with excitement and people who also care about what you care about will naturally gravitate towards you.
8.  Don’t bother yourself with the number of likes and followers you have. You need people’s hearts and genuine interests not a mere increase in numbers.
9.  **Your work will not always speak for itself.** What inspired it? What is it about? Weave your story around your work, people value works of art better when there’s a caption or story behind it to help them see the bigger picture or understand stuff better.
10. Do you want a community? You too, also join other communities and be active.
11. Grow a tough skin and don’t take it personally when people critique your work because the more you share, the more trolls 🧌 come your way.
12. Stick to things. Be consistent.

[Show Your Work! 10 Ways To Share Your Creativity And Get Discovered](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Show-Your-Work-Getting-Discovered/dp/076117897X) is a book written by [Austin Kleon](https://www.linkedin.com/company/austin-kleon/), and I picked up these lessons from reading his book.
