> I’m now on the Edinburgh Innovations Student Enterprise Board.

Today was the very first day I attended the [Edinburgh Innovations](https://edinburgh-innovations.ed.ac.uk/) Student Enterprise Board meeting because yay, I am now a member of the board. The board is simply a combination of students and alumni who are both founders and interested in the entrepreneurial process. We provide the university of Edinburgh’s student entrepreneurship support arm (Edinburgh Innovation) with information and advisory support.

## **I’ve only just heard about Mentimeter**

Nonetheless I was today years old when I discovered [Mentimeter](https://www.mentimeter.com/) (it’s a tool for everyone participating in meetings, classes, or trainings) I mean I’ve known about Mentimeter for a while … oh but I’m not sure, it did sound familiar nonetheless.

## The best way to have fun in online meetings

But today was the very first day I made use of it and I have to say that it made an almost 2-hour session absolutely fun! Now I think I know the best way to actually host online meetings. I’ve always wondered what I could do to spark the mood in long online meetings, which I don’t like anyway.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*GJsqhc36qU0xP4Hsrer6BA.png)

Now I know. Absolutely start with Mentimeter, then move a bit to Miro, and then if you want people to think, and contribute better in online brainstorming sessions, ask them more about how something makes them feel rather than just brainstorming or working with an abstract idea.

I think personalised thoughts gets people engaged more, willing to ask questions and more.
