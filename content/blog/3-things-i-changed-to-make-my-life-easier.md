I’ve been doing a few things of late that have been really interesting and I’m currently experiencing life patterns that are making each day more fulfilling. At the moment that I’m writing, these three factors make my time more productive and life easier:

1.  **I’m working a four-day week now,** which is changing how I spend my time. It isn’t a new phenomenon to me. I’ve had three-day work weeks and when I say work, I mean proper contract and employed roles. I actually have always worked every day and if it wasn’t other people’s jobs it would be for my own projects. Nonetheless, with this, I’m finding myself with these beautiful long weekends that stretch from Friday through Sunday. On these free days, I’m exploring parts of the city I never knew existed, I’m taking new courses and growing my skills. I’m practicing things and I’m dedicating time to personal projects that had been sitting on the back burner for too long. I’m also using this extra time to review work projects with a fresher mind, which is making my Monday returns to the office so much more productive.
2.  The second major change I’m experiencing is **my journey into coding**, which started when I was building Grohwie for my University of Edinburgh master’s dissertation. I’m discovering that coding isn’t just about typing mysterious commands — I’m actually having fun with it! It’s becoming like this engaging puzzle that I’m eager to solve, and this new perspective is seeping into other areas of my life too. I’m applying this problem-solving mindset to everything from my motion design work to my role as a communications manager. It’s easier to adjust pieces of code on the website that I was once too scared to touch.
3.  But perhaps the most profound change I’m implementing is my **daily gratitude practice.** I like to say 10 things I’m grateful for every day, and I’m watching how this simple act is reshaping my entire perspective. They say that gratitude is the sister to manifestation — I’m noticing that the things I express gratitude for are becoming more abundant in my life, and I’m finding myself naturally focusing on the positive aspects of each day. I’m seeing how my thoughts are creating patterns, and these patterns are weaving themselves into the fabric of my daily experiences.
