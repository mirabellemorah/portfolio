He awoke the sleeping Aphrodite in you  
For years, you have tried to suppress it  
Four years, you have tried to tame it  
Fourth year, he awoke this sleeper in you.

Now all you can think of is him  
The lover in you wants him  
Pity—you have loved him more than God  
Before him you are free  
No façades, just you  
But why awake this beast in you if he isn't ready to stay,  
Forever.

Mirabelle Morah
