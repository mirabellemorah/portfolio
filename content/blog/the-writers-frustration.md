*I'm restless... I want to write!  
I pick up my pen,I pick up my paper  
But there are no words!  
Everything is in disarray.  
Where have the words flown to?  
Why can't I write?  
What's happening to me?  
I'm losing it!  
Nothing seems to be working  
No muse is effective  
Everything is muddled up in my head  
It's like this: I scream, I write a little  
I scream some more and then I tear up the paper  
Arghh!!  
It's exhausting! I'm exhausted!!*

By [Nten Mpama](https://www.facebook.com/tenny.mpama?fref=ts)
