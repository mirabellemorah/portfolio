Rabatta

Deliverables included 1 master illustrated video advert translated into 6 language variations including Danish, Swedish, Norwegian and Dutch. Vertical & square formats provided plus editable source files for future internal updates

About company

Rabatta is a European startup with over 1.8 million users and operates a browser extension and app that auto-applies discount codes.

Need

An advert and creative direction to reach 1.8million existing customers

My process

We created a high-impact ~30-second animated video ad designed for mobile-first, sound-off environments. The video showcased Rabatta’s core benefit which is saving money effortlessly, through playful animation, bold text overlays, and engaging motion graphics featuring the brand’s quirky discount code characters. We delivered six localized versions (English, German, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Dutch), carefully adapting layouts to suit translated text while maintaining visual consistency. The project included high-quality MP4/MOV files optimized for mobile ads, plus editable source files for future updates.

Result

The final output successfully grabbed attention within the first few seconds, clearly communicated the app’s value with or without sound, and stayed true to Rabatta’s fun, friendly brand tone—ready to roll across multiple international markets.
